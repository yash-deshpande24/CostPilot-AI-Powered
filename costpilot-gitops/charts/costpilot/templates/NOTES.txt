CostPilot {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is deployed.

Open the app without exposing it to the internet:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/frontend 8090:80
  then browse to http://localhost:8090

Run a scan right now instead of waiting for the schedule:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} create job --from=cronjob/costpilot-scan scan-now
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs job/scan-now
